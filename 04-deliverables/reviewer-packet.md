# Reviewer packet specification

Status: planned, no outreach sent

## Audience

The primary reviewer is a credit, institutional risk, lending, treasury, or counterparty-risk practitioner. A technical reviewer can check reproducibility after the credit review.

## One-page brief

The brief contains:

1. The hypothetical decision and current recommendation.
2. A four-cap table with the binding constraint.
3. One collateral waterfall that separates access from price stress.
4. The strongest opposing argument.
5. One unresolved question.
6. A footer stating that MARA is a public-information case and all lending terms are fictional.

## Five-file review bundle

- `reviewer-brief.pdf`
- `credit-memo.pdf`
- `opposing-memo.pdf`
- `scenario-results.csv`
- `reproducibility.md`

The full workbook and source snapshots remain available on request. The first review request should not require a large download, wallet, account, or live demo.

## Review rubric

Ask the reviewer to score or comment on:

- whether the primary repayment analysis is decision-useful;
- whether the collateral eligibility blockers are too strict or too weak;
- whether the stress avoids double-counting;
- whether the concentration cap reflects a real shared failure mode;
- whether the opposing memo could change the decision;
- which missing diligence item matters most.

## Feedback record

Record the draft version, reviewer role, date, exact question, response, change made, and remaining disagreement. Do not name or quote a reviewer publicly without permission. Do not call silence validation.

Any outreach is a separate user-authorized action. This package drafts review materials only.
# Sensitivity review

Use the decision surface in the workbook or interactive view to compare quantity and price stress without changing the legal-gate assumption. The 4.0m USDC / 2% cell reproduces the $3.0m base recommendation. Any unresolved ownership, priority, control, or route condition supersedes the grid and produces a decline.

Use the scenario-attribution table in the interactive view to separate amount effects from hard blockers. It quantifies each recommendation and proceeds change against base and names the evidence that would resolve or mitigate the scenario; it does not estimate likelihood.
