# Credit-risk project resources

## Knowledge

- [SEC: Beginners' Guide to Financial Statements](https://www.sec.gov/about/reports-publications/investorpubsbegfinstmtguide)
  Explains the balance sheet, income statement, cash-flow statement, and shareholders' equity statement. Use before reading the MARA filing extracts.
- [SEC: How to Read a 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/answers/reada10k.htm)
  Maps the annual report, MD&A, risk factors, audited statements, notes, and auditor report. Use for issuer analysis and evidence tracing.
- [SEC: Using EDGAR to Research Investments](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-search-assistance/using-edgar-research-investments)
  Explains 10-K, 10-Q, 8-K, and Inline XBRL. Use for source acquisition and period discipline.
- [Basel Committee: Principles for the Management of Credit Risk, 2025](https://www.bis.org/bcbs/publ/d595.pdf)
  Current primary guidance on the credit environment, granting process, administration, monitoring, and controls. Use for the committee and control modules.
- [Basel Framework: General credit risk management](https://www.bis.org/committees/bcbs/basel-consolidated-guidelines/module/cri/10)
  Covers limits, independent credit review, monitoring, reporting, and control. Use for portfolio limits and governance.
- [OCC Comptroller's Handbook index](https://www.occ.treas.gov/publications-and-resources/publications/comptrollers-handbook/index-comptrollers-handbook.html)
- [Python documentation: `decimal`](https://docs.python.org/3/library/decimal.html)
  Defines exact decimal arithmetic and rounding behavior. Use for the decision engine.
- [Python documentation: `unittest`](https://docs.python.org/3/library/unittest.html)
  Defines the test framework used in the repository. Use for test reading and new invariants.
- [ECMA-376 overview: Office Open XML](https://ecma-international.org/publications-and-standards/standards/ecma-376/)
  Defines the file format underlying `.xlsx`. Use for the workbook-audit module.

## Wisdom (communities)

- A university finance, accounting, or investment club
  Use for live memo critique and a timed committee presentation.
- A professor, credit practitioner, or commercial-banking professional
  Use for independent calculation reproduction and assumption challenge. Record evidence only with permission.
- [Bogleheads forum](https://www.bogleheads.org/forum/index.php)
  A moderated finance community. Use for basic financial-statement questions, not transaction-specific legal conclusions.

- An external credit, legal, collateral, or portfolio practitioner has not provided attributable feedback.
